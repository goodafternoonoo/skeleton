Privacy policy

Rattle: Skeleton Idle RPG · Developer Yulmu · Effective November 19, 2026

한국어

Personal information we collect

The developer of Rattle: Skeleton Idle RPG (the "game") does not collect or keep any personal information: no names, contact details, location or device identifiers.

What is stored on your device

Your progress (stages, upgrades, bones, story and so on) and settings are stored on this device. Unless you use the Google Play Games save below, they are deleted when you uninstall the app.

Google Play Games save (Android)

When you are signed in to Google Play Games, the Android app also saves your progress and settings to "Saved Games" in that Google account, so you can carry on after changing phones or reinstalling. This data is kept on Google's servers and the developer cannot see it. It is handled under the Google Privacy Policy (policies.google.com/privacy). If you are not signed in, nothing is sent. You can delete the saved data in the Google Play Games app or your Google account settings.

Deleting your data

Your progress lives in two places. The progress on this device is deleted when you uninstall the app. The progress saved to your Google Play Games account is deleted when you delete this game's data in the Google Play Games app's profile settings or at play.google.com/games/profile. The developer keeps none of this data, so there is nothing to request from the developer. Send any questions to the contact address below.

Internet use

Apart from the Google Play Games save above, the game does not use the internet. It has no ads, analytics or payments.

Backup code

"Copy backup code" in the settings only turns your progress into text and copies it to the clipboard; where you keep that code is up to you. The game does not store or send the code.

Sharing with third parties

The developer collects nothing, so nothing is shared with third parties. The Google Play Games save is between your signed-in Google account and Google.

Children

The game does not collect personal information from children either.

Changes to this policy

If a feature that handles information (such as ads) is ever added, this policy will be updated before it takes effect, with notice in the game and on this page.
